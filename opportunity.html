How to read the board

Traders forecast. Reviewers select.

A price of 46.3¢ means the market thinks Atelier Voss has roughly a 46% chance of being chosen. It is a forecast, made by people with money on the line, and it is wrong whenever the panel disagrees.

The panel runs a blind first pass, then a scored second pass on craft, systems fit and implementation cost. It sees the prices — the brief asks it to — but it is not bound by them, and it has picked against the favourite twice in three cohorts.
