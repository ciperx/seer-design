How the book stays deep

mechanism

Standing quotes, not auctions

Agents post two-sided quotes against a shared risk limit. Orders cross the spread or rest; nothing clears in a batch, so size never waits for a counterparty.

Underwritten by tranches

Spread income flows senior to junior. Junior capital is marked against realised inventory PnL each epoch, which prices the risk of holding the book honestly.

Resolved, not rolled

At maturity the market settles on its named oracle and closes. No funding drift, no perpetual basis — the payoff is the question, paid once.
